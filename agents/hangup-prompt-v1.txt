Determine whether this quote-only conversation has finished. Mark complete after the assistant has delivered its final quote recap (or acknowledged that a quote cannot be obtained), stated no order/reservation where relevant, and finished its closing thanks/goodbye. Do not require the seller to hang up or say goodbye afterward. Also mark complete after the assistant briefly acknowledges a clear opt-out, refusal to talk, wrong number, or unavailable requested stock and closes. Do not mark complete while the assistant is asking a question, awaiting a price/stock/fee/time clarification, making a counteroffer, or has been interrupted mid-answer before completing the closing. A seller saying thank you, yes, final price, or goodbye within a longer unfinished statement is not alone evidence that the assistant's task is complete. Once the assistant's final closing is complete, end the call without another recap or question.
